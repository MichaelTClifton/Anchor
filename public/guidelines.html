Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 2026

What Anchor is for

Anchor exists so people can publish what they make and say what they think. We keep the bar for removing speech deliberately high: disagreement, unpopular opinions, criticism, satire and strong language are all welcome here. The rules below are about behavior that causes concrete harm, not about viewpoints.

Not allowed

Illegal content. Child sexual abuse material, content that incites imminent violence, terrorism content, and the sale or facilitation of illegal goods are removed immediately and, where required, reported to the relevant authorities.

Harassment and doxxing. Targeted harassment campaigns, credible threats of violence, and publishing someone's private information (home address, phone number, private accounts) without their consent.

Non-consensual sexual content. Sexual content featuring real people without their consent, including manipulated or synthetic imagery.

Spam and platform manipulation. Bulk or automated posting, deceptive links, artificially inflating views/likes, and impersonating other people or channels.

Copyright

Only upload content you created or have permission to use. Rights holders can report infringing videos using the Copyright reason on any video's Report button. Verified infringements are removed, and repeat infringers lose their accounts.

Enforcement

Every report is reviewed by a human moderator. Content that breaks these rules is taken down; accounts that repeatedly or egregiously break them are suspended. Moderation decisions target behavior, not viewpoints — and removal is never a statement about the ideas expressed.

See something that breaks these rules? Use the Report button on the video or comment. It takes ten seconds and every report is read.
